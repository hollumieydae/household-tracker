Privacy policy

Household Finances (the "app") is a personal household budgeting tool used by a small number of people. Last updated 29 September 2026.

What the app stores

The income, expenses, savings goals, project funds and loan details you enter are stored in your own web browser on your own device. They are not sent to the app's developer or to any server the developer controls.

Google Drive

If you choose to turn on sync, the app asks to sign in with your Google account and uses the drive.file permission. This lets the app create and open only the single household data file that you create or pick. It cannot see or change any other file in your Google Drive. The data file lives in your own Google Drive and is shared only with the people you choose to share it with.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect analytics, does not show ads, does not sell or share your data, and does not read your Google contacts, email or other Google data.

Your control

You can delete your data at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by deleting the household data file from your Google Drive. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
